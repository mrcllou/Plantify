# Plantify

🌱 Plantify

Turning forest destruction into measurable impact — and predicting future risk.

Plantify is a data-driven web platform that calculates the environmental impact of forest fires by estimating the number of trees lost and comparing it to reforestation efforts. It also includes a wildfire risk indicator to help anticipate potential forest fire danger based on environmental conditions.

🔥 Problem

Forest fires are increasing worldwide, causing massive ecological damage. However:

There is no simple way to visualize how many trees are actually lost
Reforestation progress is often unclear or untracked
Most tools focus on reporting damage, not understanding or preventing it

💡 Solution

Plantify simplifies forest fire data into a single interactive platform:

🌳 Calculates trees lost from burned area
🌱 Tracks trees replanted
📊 Shows recovery progress
⚠️ Estimates wildfire risk levels
