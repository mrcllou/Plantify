What is plantify?

"From destruction to restoration, in numbers."

Plantify is a data-driven platform that quantifies the environmental impact of forest fires by calculating how many trees are lost and comparing that loss to reforestation efforts. Instead of abstract statistics, Plantify turns environmental damage into clear, understandable numbers.
